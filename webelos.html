📚 Required Adventures (Do At Home)

These required adventures should be completed at home with family:

My Safety (Required)

Learn about safety at home, online safety, and emergency procedures. Complete safety checklist with family.

My Family (Required)

Explore family history, create family tree, learn about family traditions and values.
