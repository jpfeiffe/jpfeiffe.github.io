## FULL SCRIPT & TIMELINE

---

### 10:00-10:02 | Opening Flag Ceremony (2 min)

**YOU SAY:**  
"Good morning, Pack 584! Welcome, scouts and families! Please rise for our opening flag ceremony."

**ACTION:** Bear den presents the colors (flag ceremony)

**When ceremony completes:**  
"Thank you, Bears! Please remain standing."

---

### 10:02-10:04 | Scout Oath & Law (2 min)

**YOU SAY:**  
"Cub Scout sign!"  
*(Raise your right hand in the Cub Scout sign)*

"Repeat after me..."

**Lead the Scout Oath:**
- "On my honor..."
- "I will do my best..."
- "To do my duty to God and my country..."
- "And to obey the Scout Law..."
- "To help other people at all times..."
- "To keep myself physically strong..."
- "Mentally awake..."
- "And morally straight."

**Lead the Scout Law:**
"A Scout is..."
- "Trustworthy, Loyal, Helpful, Friendly,"
- "Courteous, Kind, Obedient, Cheerful,"
- "Thrifty, Brave, Clean, and Reverent."

**YOU SAY:**  
"Two!" *(Lower your hand)* "Please be seated."

---

### 10:04-10:08 | Loyal Theme Introduction & Run-On Skit (4 min)

**YOU SAY:**  
"This month, we're focusing on the SECOND point of the Scout Law: LOYAL."

**IMMEDIATELY:** Annie's 6 parents will run on one at a time with props:
1. "Did you say FOIL?" *(shows foil)*
2. "Did you say OIL?" *(shows oil)*
3. "Did you say BOIL?" *(shows pot)*
4. "Did you say ROYAL?" *(wears crown)*
5. "Did you say SPOIL?" *(shows spoiled item)*
6. "Did you say COIL?" *(shows coil)*

**After they finish, YOU SAY (smiling):**  
"Great guesses! But no... I said LOYAL."

---

### 10:08-10:10 | Loyal Definition (2 min)

**YOU SAY:**  
"So what does LOYAL actually mean?"

*(Pause, let 1-2 scouts answer if they want)*

"Loyal means showing firm and constant support to a person, a cause, or an ideal. It means standing by your friends, your family, your den, and your pack—not just when it's easy, but even when it takes effort."

"Can someone tell me one person or group they're loyal to?"

*(Take 2-3 quick answers from scouts: family, friends, sports team, etc.)*

"Those are all great examples! Being loyal means you stick with them and support them. That's what we're celebrating today!"

---

### 10:10-10:15 | Volunteer Recognition (5 min)

**YOU SAY:**  
"Speaking of loyal... I want to recognize some adults who show LOYALTY to our Pack by serving month after month. When I call your name, please stand."

**Call each name clearly; pause between each:**

1. **Rob Rydberg** - Committee Chair
2. **Norm Wright** - Advancements Chair
3. **Kathy Goodwin** - Treasurer
4. **Joel Odell** - Webmaster
5. **Jenny Thornton** - Outdoors/Activities Chair
6. **Annie Kyriakides** - New Member Coordinator

**After all are standing:**  
"These volunteers show what LOYAL means—they support our Pack consistently, helping make great experiences for all of you. Let's give them a BIG round of applause!"

*(Lead applause)*

"Thank you all! Please be seated."

**OPTIONAL ADD:**  
"And thank you to ALL the parents and family members who help in any way—driving, bringing snacks, cheering, setting up. Your loyalty to the scouts matters!"

---

### 10:15-10:20 | Awards Presentation (5 min)

**YOU SAY:**  
"Now it's time to celebrate YOUR loyalty—loyalty to your own growth and learning! When I call your name, please come forward."

**ACTION:**
- Call each scout's name
- Hand them their award (pin, belt loop, patch, etc.)
- Shake their hand or give high-five
- Brief praise: "Great work!" "Congratulations!" "Well done!"

**After all awards distributed:**  
"Let's give ALL our award earners a big round of applause!"

*(Lead applause; scouts return to seats)*

---

### 10:20-10:35 | STATION ROTATIONS (15 min)

**YOU SAY:**  
"Now for the fun part! We're going to split into groups and rotate through THREE activity stations. Each station is 5 minutes, so when you hear the whistle, it's time to move!"

**ACTION: Divide scouts into 3 groups**

**Method 1 - Count Off:**  
"Everyone stand up! Count off: 1, 2, 3, 1, 2, 3..." *(continue)*  
"All 1s—you're Group A! All 2s—Group B! All 3s—Group C!"

**Method 2 - By Den (simpler):**  
"Lions and Tigers—Group A!"  
"Wolves and Bears—Group B!"  
"Webelos and AOL—Group C!"

**Assign starting stations:**  
- "Group A starts at Station 1: Magic 1089!"
- "Group B starts at Station 2: Magic Paper Skit!"
- "Group C starts at Station 3: Human Knots!"

**IMPORTANT:**  
"Wolf den—you're NOT rotating. Go to your prep area with your den leader now. You'll perform your skit later!"

**YOU SAY:**  
"Go to your starting station... NOW!"

---

**10:20-10:25 | Rotation 1**  
- Watch the clock
- Walk around to stations; observe and encourage

**At 10:25 - BLOW WHISTLE:**  
"TIME! Great job! Now rotate: Group A to Station 2, Group B to Station 3, Group C to Station 1! Go!"

**10:25-10:30 | Rotation 2**  
- Continue monitoring
- Check that Wolf den is prepping their skit

**At 10:30 - BLOW WHISTLE:**  
"TIME! Last rotation: Group A to Station 3, Group B to Station 1, Group C to Station 2! Go!"

**10:30-10:35 | Rotation 3**  
- Watch the clock carefully
- At 10:34, walk toward the front to prepare for gather

**At 10:35 - BLOW WHISTLE:**  
"TIME! Everyone back to the main area! Find a seat quickly!"

---

### 10:35-10:45 | Wolf Den Skit Prep / Transition (10 min)

**YOU SAY:**  
"While the Wolf den finishes preparing their skit, let's have some fun!"

**OPTION A - If you have a volunteer for Announcement Song:**  
"Let's bring up [volunteer name] to lead us in the Announcement Song!"  
*(Volunteer leads song—see Activity-Akela-Says-Instructions.md or prep sheet)*

**OPTION B - If no volunteer:**  
"Let's do a quick cheer! When I say 'Give me an L,' you shout 'L!' Ready?"  
- "Give me an L!" → "L!"
- "Give me an O!" → "O!"
- "Give me a Y!" → "Y!"
- "Give me an A!" → "A!"
- "Give me an L!" → "L!"
- "What's that spell?" → "LOYAL!"

**Then transition to Akela Says (at ~10:40):**  
"Now let's play Akela Says!"  
*(If you have a volunteer, let them lead; otherwise, YOU lead—see Activity-Akela-Says-Instructions.md)*

---

### 10:45-10:48 | Jokes (3 min)

**YOU SAY:**  
"We have some scouts and adults with jokes to share! Listen carefully—these are funny!"

**ACTION:**  
- A volunteer coordinates 4 scout/adult pairs telling jokes (see **Activity-Jokes-Instructions.md**)
- Each pair tells a quick question-and-answer joke
- Pause for laughter after each joke

**IF NO VOLUNTEER:**  
- You can skip this activity entirely (save 3 minutes)
- OR call up pairs yourself using the jokes in Activity-Jokes-Instructions.md

**YOU SAY (after jokes complete):**  
"Thank you, joke tellers! Let's give them a hand!"

---

### 10:48-10:51 | Wolf Den Skit (3 min)

**YOU SAY:**  
"And now, the moment we've been waiting for... the Wolf Den skit! Let's give them a warm welcome!"

**ACTION:**  
- Wolf den performs their skit
- Smile, laugh, encourage!

**After skit:**  
"Excellent job, Wolf Den! Let's give them a BIG round of applause!"

*(Lead enthusiastic applause)*

**YOU SAY:**  
"Before we close, I have some important information for families:"

1. **Activity Shirts:** "If you're a new scout (Tiger or older) and have paid your dues, see Susan Cook to order your Pack activity shirt."

**YOU SAY:**  
"Questions? Catch me after the meeting! Now, for our closing thought..."

---

### 10:54-10:57 | Cubmaster's Minute (3 min)

**YOU SAY (thoughtfully, making eye contact):**

"A Scout is Loyal... though not blindly so."

"The original description of the second point of the Scout Law from way back in 1911 said, 'A Scout is loyal to all those to whom loyalty is due.'"

"Loyalty is a two-way street. And it requires courage sometimes, too—because a Scout is also BRAVE. Loyalty means remaining steadfast in your support even when someone stumbles. It means helping them do better, be better—because a Scout is HELPFUL."

"Loyalty is prominently represented by the BLUE in the BSA logo."

*(Point to any BSA logo visible, or to the flag)*

"Let that loyalty be prominent in YOU as well."

"Remember: **A Scout is Loyal.**"

*(Pause for 2 seconds)*

"Thank you all for being here today and showing loyalty to your Pack!"

## Quick Time-Recovery Options

**If running BEHIND schedule:**

| Behind by... | Cut or shorten... |
|--------------|-------------------|
| 2-3 minutes | Shorten jokes to 2 pairs instead of 4 |
| 4-5 minutes | Skip Akela Says; go straight to jokes |
| 6+ minutes | Cut jokes entirely; skip transition games |

**If running AHEAD of schedule:**

| Ahead by... | Extend... |
|-------------|-----------|
| 2-3 minutes | Let stations run an extra minute each |
| 4-5 minutes | Add more Akela Says rounds or let scouts share their own jokes |

---

## Emergency Backup Plans

**Volunteer doesn't show:**
- Station 1 (1089): YOU can run it—it's fully scripted
- Station 2 (Paper Skit): Any parent can do it with the script
- Station 3 (Human Knots): Super simple; any adult can facilitate

**Wolf den skit falls through:**
- Skip it; go straight to announcements
- Or have Wolf den lead a quick cheer instead

**Running very late:**
- Keep: Opening flags, Oath/Law, theme, awards, Cubmaster Minute, closing flags
- Cut: Run-on skit, jokes, Akela Says, transition activities

---

## Voice & Presence Tips

✅ **Project your voice** - Make sure the back row can hear  
✅ **Make eye contact** - Connect with scouts and families  
✅ **Smile and have fun** - Your energy sets the tone!  
✅ **Use the Cub Scout sign** to get attention when it's noisy  
✅ **Keep moving** - Don't let any segment drag  
✅ **Celebrate everyone** - Praise scouts, volunteers, families
