## STATION ROTATION MANAGEMENT

**How to rotate groups:**
1. At 10:20am, announce: "Scouts, we're dividing into 3 groups for stations!"
2. Count off: "Group A... Group B... Group C..."
3. Assign each group to starting station
4. Blow whistle at 10:25 and 10:30 for rotations
5. Call rotation directions clearly: "Group A to Station 2, Group B to Station 3, Group C to Station 1!"

**Wolf Den Exception:** Wolf den does NOT rotate; they go directly to prep area at 10:20.
