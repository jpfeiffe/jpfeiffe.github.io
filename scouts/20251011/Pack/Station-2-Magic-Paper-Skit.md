# Station 2: The Magic Paper Skit

**Station Duration:** 5 minutes per rotation (3 rotations total)  
**Group Size:** 10-12 scouts per rotation  
**Location:** Table or small performance area

---

## Your Role

You'll perform a fun comedy skit with a volunteer where you "trick" them with wordplay. This is a quick, repeatable performance that keeps scouts laughing and engaged!

---

## Materials Provided

- [ ] 5-6 large sheets of paper (8.5x11 or larger)
- [ ] 5-6 quarters (coins)
- [ ] Table or performance space

**Check before each rotation:** Make sure you have enough paper and quarters for this group!

---

## The Skit Script

You'll need TWO people:
- **Magician** (You, the station leader)
- **Spectator** (A volunteer scout from the group)

### Step-by-Step Script

**MAGICIAN:**  
"Today I'm going to give this lucky audience member the chance to earn some money!"

*(Hold up a large sheet of paper dramatically)*

"Please observe this sheet of MAGIC paper. If you can tear this magic paper into FOUR equal pieces, I will give you... a QUARTER!"

*(Pause for effect)*

**SPECTATOR:**  
"That's simple!"

*(The spectator takes the paper and folds it in half, then folds it in half again, then tears along the folds)*

"See? I made four equal pieces!"

*(Hold up the four pieces triumphantly)*

**MAGICIAN:**  
*(Count the pieces dramatically)* "One... two... three... FOUR equal pieces! Excellent! And here... is your quarter."

*(Hand the spectator just ONE of the torn paper squares instead of a coin)*

**SPECTATOR:**  
"You tricked me!"

**MAGICIAN:**  
*(Grin)* "Yes, well, magicians know lots of tricks!"

*(Optional: Then hand them a real quarter as a thank-you for playing along)*

---

## How to Run This Station (5 Minutes)

### Before Scouts Arrive (30 seconds)
- Have paper and quarters visible on table
- Know which scout you'll ask to volunteer first

### When Scouts Arrive (30 seconds)
"Welcome to Station 2: The Magic Paper Skit! I'm going to show you a magic trick, and I need a volunteer!"

### Perform the Skit (2 minutes)
1. Ask for a volunteer
2. Follow the script above
3. Emphasize the punchline (giving a paper square, not a coin)
4. Everyone should laugh!
5. Optional: Give the real quarter afterward

### Let Scouts Try (2 minutes)
"Now YOU try it!"
- Split the group into pairs
- Give each pair a sheet of paper
- Have them practice: one person is Magician, one is Spectator
- Walk around and help them with the script
- They can switch roles if time allows

### Wrap-Up (30 seconds)
"Great job, magicians! The trick is the word 'quarter' can mean two things!"

---

## Tips for Success

✅ **Exaggerate Your Performance:** Be dramatic! Use funny voices! Make it silly!

✅ **Pause for Laughter:** After you hand over the paper square, wait for scouts to laugh before continuing

✅ **Be Clear About the Trick:** It's a word trick—"quarter" can mean a coin OR one-fourth of something

✅ **Encourage Participation:** Let scouts try it themselves; they'll remember it better

✅ **Keep Energy High:** This is meant to be FUN! Smile and laugh with them

✅ **It's Okay to Improvise:** If the volunteer adds their own reaction, go with it!

---

## Common Questions & Answers

**Q: "Can I be the magician?"**  
A: "Yes! After I show you how it works, you can practice with a partner!"

**Q: "Do we get to keep the quarters?"**  
A: "The first volunteer might! But the quarters are for the skit, so we need them back for the next group."

**Q: "What if I mess up the folds?"**  
A: "That's okay! The trick still works as long as you tear the paper into pieces. It doesn't have to be perfect!"

**Q: "Is this real magic?"**  
A: "It's word magic! The trick is that 'quarter' means TWO things—a coin and one-fourth. That's what makes it funny!"

---

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Volunteer is shy | Demonstrate yourself first, then ask them to help you repeat it |
| Paper tears wrong | Say "Even better—magical irregular pieces!" and continue |
| Ran out of quarters | The joke works even if you just pretend to hand them a quarter (mime it) |
| Too much time left | Let more scouts volunteer to be the magician or spectator |
| Running out of time | Skip the "pairs practice" part; just do the demo once |
| Scout doesn't get the joke | Explain: "A quarter is 25 cents, but a quarter ALSO means one-fourth. I gave you one-fourth of the paper!" |

---

## Variations (If You Want to Mix It Up)

**Variation 1: Let a Scout Be the Magician**  
After you demo it once, let a confident scout volunteer to perform it for the group!

**Variation 2: Everyone Does It at Once**  
Give each scout a sheet of paper, have them fold and tear, then reveal that you all made "quarters"

**Variation 3: Add Extra Drama**  
Wear a silly hat, use a "magic wand" (pencil), say "abracadabra" before the reveal

---

## The Teaching Moment

After the skit, you can say:
- "This trick teaches us to listen carefully to words—they can have more than one meaning!"
- "A good Scout is TRUSTWORTHY—but it's okay to have fun with word games!"
- Optional connection to theme: "The paper stayed loyal to being torn into quarters—just not the kind of quarter you expected!"

---

## Rotation Transition

**When Time is Called (at 5-minute mark):**
1. "Great job, everyone! You're all magicians now!"
2. Collect any leftover quarters (you'll need them for the next group)
3. Gather unused paper
4. Welcome the next group with the same energy!

---

## Quick Reference: The Core Joke

**Setup:** "Tear this paper into four pieces and I'll give you a quarter"  
**Action:** Scout tears paper into four pieces  
**Punchline:** Magician gives them ONE of the four pieces (a "quarter" of the paper, not a coin)  
**Reveal:** It's a word trick! Quarter = coin OR one-fourth

---

## Sample Dialogue (Simplified Version)

**You:** "I'll give you a quarter if you tear this paper into 4 pieces!"  
**Scout:** *(tears paper)* "Done!"  
**You:** *(give them one paper square)* "Here's your quarter!"  
**Scout:** "Hey!"  
**You:** "That IS a quarter—one-fourth of the paper! Got ya!"
