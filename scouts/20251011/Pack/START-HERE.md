## 🎯 Success Tips

1. **Prep the Night Before:** Gather all materials, confirm all volunteers
2. **Brief Volunteers Well:** Give them instruction sheets, answer questions
3. **Use a Whistle/Timer:** Keep to 60-minute schedule - it goes fast!
4. **Model Silliness:** Adults show kids it's OK to be fun and goofy (Karl's goal!)
5. **Start and End on Time:** Parents appreciate punctuality
6. **Thank Everyone:** Public recognition encourages future participation
7. **Take Photos:** Document the fun for families
8. **Have Fun!** Your energy sets the tone for the scouts

---

## ⚠️ Common Pitfalls to Avoid

1. **Don't skip volunteer recognition** - It connects to the Loyal theme!
2. **Don't let activities run long** - Stick to the timeline
3. **Don't forget Wolf den prep time** - They need 10 minutes at 10:35am
4. **Don't rush Cubmaster's Minute** - It's the meaningful closing message
5. **Don't forget to brief Annie's 6 parents** - They need to know when to run on
6. **Don't forget quarters** - You need 4 for the Magic Paper skit punchline
