# Jokes Activity - Leader Instructions

**Activity:** Scout/Adult Joke Pairs  
**Time:** 10:45-10:48am (3 minutes)  
**Location:** Front of room (full group gathered)  
**Your Role:** Coordinate 4 scout/adult pairs to tell jokes

---

## Overview

You'll be organizing 4 pairs (8 people total) to tell quick jokes to the whole pack. Each pair has a simple question-and-answer joke. This is a fun way to involve scouts and adults together!

---

## Your Responsibilities

### Before the Meeting (Prep):
- [ ] Recruit 4 scouts and 4 adults to be joke pairs
- [ ] Assign each pair their joke (see below)
- [ ] Brief them: "It's just a quick question and answer—keep it simple and fun!"
- [ ] Let them know they'll go up around 10:45am

### During the Meeting:
- [ ] Stay near the front starting at 10:45am
- [ ] When the Cubmaster calls for jokes, help pairs come forward one at a time
- [ ] After each joke, pause for laughter/applause
- [ ] Keep it moving—about 45 seconds per pair

---

---

## Tips for Success

✅ **Keep pairs ready:** Have them sitting near the front so they can come up quickly  
✅ **Encourage silliness:** These are corny jokes—lean into it!  
✅ **Pause for laughter:** Give the audience a moment to react after each punchline  
✅ **Don't rush:** 45 seconds per pair is plenty of time  
✅ **Model enthusiasm:** Your energy helps scouts feel comfortable  

---

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| **Scout is shy/nervous** | Let the adult do the setup question; scout just says punchline |
| **Can't recruit 4 pairs** | Use 2-3 pairs instead; adjust timing with Cubmaster |
| **Joke falls flat** | Lead enthusiastic applause anyway—scouts tried their best! |
| **Running behind schedule** | Cubmaster may cut to 2 pairs—be flexible |
| **Scout forgets punchline** | Quietly prompt them or have adult finish |

---

## Backup Plan

**If you can't find volunteers:**  
- Cubmaster can skip this activity entirely (saves 3 minutes)
- OR Cubmaster can tell 1-2 jokes solo
- OR invite scouts to spontaneously share their own jokes

**If running behind:**  
- Cut to 2 pairs instead of 4 (saves 90 seconds)

**If running ahead:**  
- Invite 1-2 scouts to share their own jokes (if time allows)
