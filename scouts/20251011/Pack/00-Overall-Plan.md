# Pack Meeting - Overall Plan

**Date:** Saturday, October 11, 2025  
**Theme:** Loyal (Scout Law Point #2)
**Location:** Indoor  
**Pack Size:** All dens (Lions, Tigers, Wolves, Bears, Webelos, AOL)  
**Meeting Time:** 60 minutes  
**Adult Leaders Needed:** ~10 (Cubmaster + 3 Station Helpers + Den Leaders + Volunteers)

## Meeting Overview

This Pack meeting teaches the meaning of "Loyal" (2nd point of Scout Law) through a mix of full-group ceremonies, parallel activity stations, and a featured Wolf den skit. The meeting uses stations to keep scouts engaged while accommodating a large, multi-den group.

## Meeting Structure

### Opening (15 minutes) - FULL GROUP
- Opening flag ceremony (Bears)
- Scout Oath & Law recitation
- Theme introduction: "Loyal"
- Loyal run-on skit (parents with props)
- Loyal definition teaching
- Volunteer recognition (connecting to theme)
- Awards presentation

### Station Rotations (15 minutes) - SMALL GROUPS
Scouts divided into 3 groups, rotating through 3 stations (5 minutes each):
- **Station 1:** Magic 1089 Number Trick
- **Station 2:** Magic Paper Skit
- **Station 3:** Human Knots

### Wolf Den Skit Prep (10 minutes) - SEPARATED
- Wolf den prepares their skit in separate space
- Other scouts remain at stations or gather for transition activities

### Wrap-Up Activities (15 minutes) - FULL GROUP
- Announcement song (gathering transition)
- Akela Says game
- Jokes by scout/adult pairs
- Wolf den skit performance

### Closing (5 minutes) - FULL GROUP
- Announcements
- Cubmaster's Minute on Loyalty
- Closing flag ceremony (Webelos)

## Theme Integration: Loyal

The theme is woven throughout:
1. **Run-on skit:** Humor around word "loyal" (foil, oil, etc.)
2. **Definition:** Clear teaching of what loyalty means
3. **Volunteer recognition:** Adults who show loyal service
4. **Cubmaster's Minute:** Deep reflection on loyalty's meaning
