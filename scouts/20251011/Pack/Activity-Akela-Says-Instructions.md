# Akela Says Game - Leader Instructions

**Time Slot:** Part of wrap-up activities (~10:45am, 2-3 minutes)  
**Leader:** Adult Volunteer  
**Group:** Whole Pack (all scouts)  
**Purpose:** Fun listening game; gets scouts moving; transitions energy before jokes and skit

---

## What is "Akela Says"?

This is the Cub Scout version of "Simon Says"! Akela is a symbolic leadership figure in Cub Scouting (from The Jungle Book), so we use "Akela Says" instead of "Simon Says."

**The Rule:** Scouts only perform the action if you say "Akela says..." first. If you don't say "Akela says," they should NOT do the action!

---

## Setup (30 seconds)

1. Ask scouts to stand up and spread out (arm's length apart)
2. Get their attention: "We're going to play Akela Says!"
3. Explain the rule clearly: "Only do the action if I say 'Akela says' first. If I don't say it, freeze!"
4. Optional: Do one practice round so they understand

---

## How to Play

**Call out commands quickly** (about 5-10 seconds between each):
- Some with "Akela says..." (they should do it)
- Some WITHOUT "Akela says..." (they should NOT do it)

**Two Options for Elimination:**
1. **Cooperative Mode (Recommended for Pack Meeting):** Everyone keeps playing even if they make a mistake. Just smile and keep going!
2. **Elimination Mode:** If a scout does an action without hearing "Akela says," they sit down. Last person standing wins.

**Tip:** For a large pack meeting with limited time, use Cooperative Mode to keep everyone engaged!

---

## Suggested Commands (Pick 8-12)

### Easy Commands (Start Here)
- "Akela says touch your nose!"
- "Akela says pat your head!"
- "Akela says jump once!"
- "Akela says show the Cub Scout sign!" ✌️
- "Akela says give yourself a hug!"
- "Akela says touch your toes!"
- "Akela says spin in a circle!"

### Trick Commands (No "Akela Says")
- "Sit down!" (They should NOT sit)
- "Put your hands on your hips!" (They should NOT do it)
- "Touch your ears!" (They should NOT do it)
- "Wave hello!" (They should NOT wave)

### Scout-Themed Commands
- "Akela says do the Cub Scout salute!" 🫡
- "Akela says show the Scout handshake position!"
- "Akela says howl like a wolf!" (Awoooo!)
- "Akela says flap like an eagle!"
- "Akela says stand at attention!"
- "Akela says freeze like a statue!"

### Fun/Silly Commands
- "Akela says hop on one foot!"
- "Akela says make a silly face!"
- "Akela says wiggle like a worm!"
- "Akela says balance on your tiptoes!"
- "Akela says march in place!"
- "Akela says give a thumbs up!"

---

## Sample Game Sequence (2-3 minutes)

1. "Akela says stand up straight!"
2. "Akela says touch your shoulders!"
3. "Touch your knees!" ❌ (No "Akela says" - trick!)
4. "Akela says show the Cub Scout sign!"
5. "Akela says jump twice!"
6. "Sit down!" ❌ (Another trick!)
7. "Akela says pat yourself on the back!"
8. "Akela says give a quiet howl!"
9. "Wave your arms!" ❌ (Final trick!)
10. "Akela says freeze like a statue!" (Good ending command)

---

## Tips for Success

✅ **Start Slow, Speed Up:** Begin with obvious "Akela says" commands so scouts get the rhythm, then add tricks

✅ **Use Your Voice:** Say "Akela says" very clearly. Make the trick commands sound different or sneaky!

✅ **Demonstrate at First:** For the first few commands, DO the action yourself so scouts can see

✅ **Then Stop Demonstrating:** After a few rounds, stop showing the action—make them listen only!

✅ **Keep Energy High:** Speak with enthusiasm! "Akela says... JUMP!"

✅ **Watch for Mistakes:** When someone does a trick command, smile and say "Ooh, I tricked you! Keep playing!"

✅ **End on a Calm Note:** Last command should be something settling like "freeze" or "sit quietly"

## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** Scouts don't understand the rules  
**Solution:** Do 2-3 practice rounds: "This is practice! Akela says touch your head!" Then explain again.

**Problem:** Too noisy/chaotic  
**Solution:** Use the Cub Scout sign (raise your hand) and wait for quiet before continuing

**Problem:** Scouts are arguing about who's out  
**Solution:** Switch to Cooperative Mode: "Everyone keeps playing no matter what!"

**Problem:** Running short on time  
**Solution:** Do only 6-8 commands and end quickly with "Akela says take a bow!"

**Problem:** Have extra time  
**Solution:** Do a second round, or let a scout volunteer lead a few commands!

---

## Ending the Game

**Good Ending Commands:**
- "Akela says freeze like a statue!"
- "Akela says sit down quietly and give yourself a round of applause!"
- "Akela says get ready for jokes—big smiles!"

Then transition: "Great job listening! Now we have some funny jokes for you..."
