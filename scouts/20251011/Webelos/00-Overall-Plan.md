# Bobcat Webelos Adventure - Overall Meeting Plan

**Date:** Saturday, October 11, 2025  
**Location:** Indoor  
**Den Size:** 18 Scouts  
**Meeting Time:** 75 minutes  
**Adult Leaders Needed:** 5 (1 Den Leader + 4 Station Helpers)

## Meeting Overview

This meeting completes the Bobcat Adventure for Webelos Scouts. The meeting uses a station rotation model to accommodate 18 scouts effectively.

## Meeting Structure

### Opening (10 minutes) - FULL GROUP
- Welcome & attendance
- Pledge of Allegiance
- Introduction to Bobcat Adventure
- Assign scouts to rotation groups (A, B, C, D - 4-5 scouts each)

### Station Rotations (48 minutes) - SMALL GROUPS
Four 12-minute rotations:
- **Station 1:** Scout Law Detective (Requirement 3)
- **Station 2:** Teach Sign, Salute & Handshake (Requirement 6)
- **Station 3:** Denner Brainstorming (Requirement 5)
- **Station 4:** Silent Line Up (Requirement 1)

### Full Group Activities (15 minutes)
- **Activity 1:** Recite Scout Oath & Law (Requirement 2) - 8 min
- **Activity 2:** Create Den Code of Conduct (Requirement 4) - 4 min
- **Activity 3:** Finalize Denner Responsibilities (Requirement 5) - 3 min

### Closing (2 minutes) - FULL GROUP
- Review accomplishments
- Announcements
- Closing ceremony

**Station Areas:** Ensure adequate spacing between stations (at least 10-15 feet)

- Station 3: Table or quiet corner for writing
