# Station 1: Scout Law Detective

**Requirement:** Requirement 3 - Learn About the Scout Law  
**Time:** 12 minutes per rotation (4 rotations total)  
**Group Size:** 4-5 scouts per rotation  
**Location:** Table or floor space

---

## Your Role

You'll facilitate a fun memory game that helps scouts learn all 12 points of the Scout Law. This is an interactive activity where scouts identify which Scout Law point is missing from a set of cards.

---

## Materials Provided

- [ ] 12 index cards (one set) with Scout Law points written on them:
  1. Trustworthy
  2. Loyal
  3. Helpful
  4. Friendly
  5. Courteous
  6. Kind
  7. Obedient
  8. Cheerful
  9. Thrifty
  10. Brave
  11. Clean
  12. Reverent
- [ ] Extra cards (in case one gets lost)
- [ ] Webelos handbook (for scout reference)

**Setup Check:** Make sure you have all 12 cards before each rotation starts!

---

## Activity Instructions

### Before Scouts Arrive (2 minutes)
1. Spread all 12 cards face-up on the table in a random arrangement
2. Make sure cards are visible and spaced out

### When Scouts Arrive at Your Station (1 minute)
1. Welcome the group: "Hi everyone! You're at Station 1: Scout Law Detective!"
2. Quick intro: "We're going to play a memory game to help you learn the 12 points of the Scout Law."

### The Game - Repeat Multiple Times (10 minutes)

**Round Setup:**
1. Have all 12 cards face-up on the table
2. Tell scouts: "Look carefully at all the cards. You have 30 seconds to study them."
3. Count down: "30 seconds... 20 seconds... 10 seconds... Time's up!"

**The Challenge:**
4. "Now turn around so you can't see the table!" (Make sure no peeking!)
5. Quickly remove ONE card and hide it
6. Mix up the remaining 11 cards so they're in different positions
7. "Okay, turn back around!"
8. "Which point of the Scout Law is missing?"

**Let Scouts Discuss:**
- Scouts can work together to figure it out
- They can use their handbook if needed
- Give them 30-60 seconds to discuss

**Reveal:**
9. Show the missing card
10. Brief discussion: "What does [that point] mean?" (Let scouts share ideas)

**Repeat:** Do this 5-6 times in the 12 minutes, removing different cards each time

### Last Few Minutes (2 minutes)
If you finish early or have extra time:
- Have scouts recite all 12 points together
- Quiz each other: "Can you name all 12 in order?"
- Ask: "Which Scout Law point do you think is most important? Why?"

---

## Tips for Success

✅ **Keep the Energy Up:** Make it exciting! "Ooh, this is a tough one!"

✅ **Praise Participation:** "Great teamwork!" or "I love how you're helping each other!"

✅ **It's Okay to Reference the Handbook:** Learning is the goal, not memorization under pressure

✅ **Vary Difficulty:** 
   - Start with easier ones (like "Friendly" or "Helpful")
   - Progress to harder ones (like "Reverent" or "Thrifty")

✅ **Make it Personal:** "Can you think of a time when you were brave?"

---

## Common Questions & Answers

**Q: "What if a scout doesn't know what a word means?"**  
A: Great learning opportunity! Ask the other scouts first, then explain simply:
- **Trustworthy:** Telling the truth and keeping promises
- **Loyal:** Standing by your friends and family
- **Courteous:** Being polite and respectful
- **Thrifty:** Not wasting things; being careful with resources
- **Reverent:** Respecting beliefs and faith

**Q: "What if they get it wrong?"**  
A: No problem! Say "Not quite, let's look again together" and help them figure it out.

**Q: "What if we finish early?"**  
A: Play more rounds, or have them challenge each other by removing 2 cards at once!

**Q: "What if a card gets lost?"**  
A: Use an extra card or write the missing point on a piece of paper.

## Rotation Transition

**When Time is Called:**
1. Thank the scouts: "Great job, detectives!"
2. Collect all 12 cards (count them!)
3. Reset the cards on the table for the next group
4. Welcome the next rotation
