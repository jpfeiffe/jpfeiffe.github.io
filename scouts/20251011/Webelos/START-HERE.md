## 🎯 Success Tips

1. **Prep the Night Before:** Make the Scout Law cards ahead of time
2. **Brief Volunteers Well:** Give them their instruction sheets early
3. **Use a Whistle/Timer:** Keep rotations on schedule - call time every 12 minutes!
4. **Keep Groups Moving:** 4 stations means everyone stays engaged
5. **Stay on Schedule:** 75 minutes goes fast with 4 rotations!
6. **Celebrate Progress:** "You're completing your Bobcat Adventure today!"
7. **Take Photos:** Parents love to see what scouts are doing
8. **Have Fun!** Your energy sets the tone
