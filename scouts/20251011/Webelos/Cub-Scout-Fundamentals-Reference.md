## 1. CUB SCOUT SIGN ✌️

### When to Use:
- To get attention during meetings
- When reciting the Scout Oath or Scout Law
- To show you're ready and listening

### How to Do It:
1. Raise your **RIGHT hand** to shoulder height
2. Make a **peace sign** with index and middle fingers straight up
3. Use your **thumb** to hold down your ring finger and pinky
4. Keep your arm at **shoulder level**

### What It Means:
The two fingers stand for:
- **Helping other people**
- **Obeying the Scout Law**

```
     ✌️
    /|\
     |
    / \
```

---

## 2. CUB SCOUT SALUTE 🫡

### When to Use:
- During the Pledge of Allegiance
- When the flag passes by
- To show respect and honor
- At ceremonies

### How to Do It:
1. Start with the **Cub Scout sign** (same hand position)
2. Bring your hand up and touch your **forehead**
3. Tips of index and middle fingers touch your forehead
4. Keep the same finger position

### What It Means:
A salute shows respect and honor

```
    ✌️🫡
    /|\
     |
    / \
```

---

## 3. CUB SCOUT HANDSHAKE 🤝

### When to Use:
- Greeting other Cub Scouts
- Meeting Scout leaders
- At ceremonies and special events
- Whenever you meet a fellow Scout

### How to Do It:
1. Use your **RIGHT hand** (the same hand as the sign and salute)
2. Shake hands normally with right hands
3. **Extend** your index and middle fingers
4. Place them along the **inside** of the other person's wrist

### What It Means:
- The two extended fingers remind us to help others and obey the Scout Law

```
   👉🤝👈
   (Right hands!)
```

---

## A Note on Right Hand vs. Left Hand

This is a common point of confusion!
- **Cub Scouts** (Lions, Tigers, Wolves, Bears, Webelos) use the **RIGHT-HANDED** handshake.
- **Scouts BSA** (older youth, formerly Boy Scouts) use the traditional **LEFT-HANDED** handshake.

You are learning the official Cub Scout handshake today!

## Practice Tips

✅ **Sign:** Practice at home - use it to get your family's attention!

✅ **Salute:** Practice during the Pledge of Allegiance at school (just use the Cub Scout version)

✅ **Handshake:** Practice with family members using your right hand

✅ **Remember:** Right hand for sign, salute, AND handshake in Cub Scouts!

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Using the left hand (that's for older Scouts!)  
✅ Right hand for the Cub Scout handshake

❌ Fingers not extended on handshake  
✅ Index and middle fingers go along the wrist

❌ Arm too high or too low on the sign  
✅ Keep arm at shoulder height

❌ Wrong fingers up  
✅ Index and middle fingers up, thumb holds down the other two

## Parent Note

These three fundamentals are core skills that your Cub Scout will use at every meeting. Practice them at home! You can:
- Have them show you each one
- Use the sign at home to get their attention
- Practice the handshake together (it feels weird at first!)
- Ask them what the two fingers represent
