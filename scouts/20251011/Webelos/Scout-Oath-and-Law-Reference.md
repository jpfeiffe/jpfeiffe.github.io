# Scout Oath and Scout Law - Reference Sheet

**Print this for easy reference during the meeting**

---

## THE SCOUT OATH

**On my honor I will do my best**

**To do my duty to God and my country**  
**and to obey the Scout Law;**

**To help other people at all times;**

**To keep myself physically strong,**  
**mentally awake, and morally straight.**

---

## THE THREE POINTS OF THE SCOUT OATH

### 1. Duty to God and My Country
- Follow the teachings of your family and faith
- Be a good citizen
- Obey the laws
- Work for your country's good
- Live by the Scout Law

### 2. Duty to Other People
- Help others when they need it
- Do a Good Turn daily
- Be kind and helpful
- Make the world a better place

### 3. Duty to Self
- **Physically Strong:** Take care of your body, eat healthy, exercise
- **Mentally Awake:** Keep learning, be curious, ask questions
- **Morally Straight:** Be honest, make good choices, have strong character

---

## THE SCOUT LAW

**A Scout is:**

1. **TRUSTWORTHY** - Tell the truth and keep promises
2. **LOYAL** - Be true to family, friends, and leaders
3. **HELPFUL** - Help others without expecting reward
4. **FRIENDLY** - Be a friend to everyone
5. **COURTEOUS** - Be polite and respectful
6. **KIND** - Treat others the way you want to be treated
7. **OBEDIENT** - Follow rules and do your duty
8. **CHEERFUL** - Look for the bright side of things
9. **THRIFTY** - Don't waste resources; save for the future
10. **BRAVE** - Face difficulty with courage
11. **CLEAN** - Keep body, mind, and words clean
12. **REVERENT** - Be respectful of faith and beliefs

## QUICK MEMORY TIPS

### For the Scout Oath:
Think of it in three parts:
1. **Opening:** "On my honor I will do my best"
2. **The Three Duties:** God & Country, Other People, Self
3. **Personal Promise:** Physically strong, mentally awake, morally straight

### For the Scout Law (12 points):
**Memory trick - First Letters:**
**T-L-H-F-C-K-O-C-T-B-C-R**

Or group them:
- **Personal Character:** Trustworthy, Loyal, Helpful, Friendly
- **How We Act:** Courteous, Kind, Obedient, Cheerful
- **Life Skills:** Thrifty, Brave, Clean, Reverent

## WHEN DO WE USE THESE?

### Scout Oath:
- Beginning of every meeting
- Flag ceremonies
- Special occasions and ceremonies
- Earning rank awards

### Scout Law:
- Beginning of meetings (often after the Oath)
- To guide behavior and decisions
- When discussing what it means to be a Scout
- As a reminder of Scout values

### The Cub Scout Sign:
- Always make the Cub Scout sign when reciting the Oath or Law
- Right hand up, index and middle fingers up, thumb holds down other two fingers

## DISCUSSION QUESTIONS FOR SCOUTS

**About the Oath:**
- What does "On my honor" mean to you?
- How can you show duty to other people this week?
- What does it mean to be "mentally awake"?

**About the Law:**
- Which point of the Scout Law is easiest for you? Why?
- Which point is hardest? Why?
- Can you think of a time when you were brave?
- How can you be helpful at home?

## FOR LEADERS

### Teaching Tips:
- Recite together as a group - it builds confidence
- Don't expect perfection - learning takes time
- Use the handbook back cover as reference
- Connect to real-life examples
- Praise effort and progress

### Practice Activities:
- Scout Law Detective (memory game with cards)
- Scout Law charades (act out each point)
- Scout Law relay (race to say them in order)
- Create posters for each point
- Discuss real-life examples
