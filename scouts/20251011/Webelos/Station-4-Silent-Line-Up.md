# Station 4: Silent Line Up

**Requirement:** Requirement 1 - Get to Know Members of Your Den  
**Time:** 12 minutes per rotation (4 rotations total)  
**Group Size:** 4-5 scouts per rotation  
**Location:** Open space (at least 15 feet clear)

---

## Your Role

You'll lead scouts through a fun challenge where they line up in various orders WITHOUT TALKING! This teaches communication, teamwork, and helps scouts learn about each other.

---

## Materials Provided

- [ ] Clear floor space (at least 15 feet)
- [ ] Optional: Small whiteboard and marker (if scouts want to write to communicate)

**No special materials needed - just space and scouts!**

---

## Activity Instructions

### Before Scouts Arrive (1 minute)
1. Make sure your area is clear of obstacles
2. Identify the start and end points for the line
3. Familiarize yourself with the challenge ideas below

### When Scouts Arrive at Your Station (1 minute)
1. Welcome: "Hi everyone! You're at Station 4: Silent Line Up!"
2. Quick intro: "You're going to line up in different orders, but here's the catch - NO TALKING!"

### Introduce the Challenge (1 minute)

**Explain the Rules:**
"You need to figure out how to line up without using words. BUT you CAN use:"
- Hand gestures
- Pointing
- Miming/acting things out
- Sign language (if you know it)
- Writing in the air
- Nodding/shaking your head

**Ask:** "Can you show me a few ways to communicate without talking?"
- Let scouts demonstrate (1-2 examples)
- "Great! Let's try it!"

### Round 1: Birth Month (3 minutes)

**The Challenge:**
"Line up in order by your birth month - January on this end, December on that end. No talking - GO!"

**Your Role:**
- Start timing
- Watch them figure it out (don't help unless they're completely stuck!)
- It will be chaotic and fun - that's okay!
- Give hints only if needed: "Maybe use your fingers to show the month number?"

**Check the Results:**
Once they think they're done (or after 2-3 minutes):
- "Okay, let's see if you got it!"
- Have each scout call out their birth month down the line
- Celebrate success OR "Almost! That was tricky!"

### Round 2: Alphabetically by First Name (3 minutes)

**The Challenge:**
"Now line up alphabetically by your FIRST name - A to Z. Remember, no talking!"

**Your Role:**
- This is usually easier than birth month
- Watch how they've improved their communication
- Encourage creative solutions

**Check the Results:**
- Have each scout say their first name down the line
- "Great job! You're getting better at this!"

### Round 3: Choose Based on Time (3 minutes)

**If You Have 4-5 Scouts:**
Pick ONE of these challenges:

**Option A: By Age (Youngest to Oldest)**
- "Line up from youngest to oldest"
- Check by having them call out their age

**Option B: By Last Name (Alphabetically)**
- "Line up by your last name - A to Z"
- Check by having them say their last name

**Option C: By Height (Shortest to Tallest)**
- "Line up from shortest to tallest"
- This one is easier since they can see!

**Option D: By Number of Letters in First Name**
- "Line up by how many letters are in your first name - fewest to most"
- Example: Jo (2) → Alex (4) → Sarah (5) → Michael (7)
- This is HARD but fun!

### Debrief (1-2 minutes)

**Ask Discussion Questions:**
- "What was the hardest part?"
- "How did you communicate without words?"
- "What did you learn about each other?"
- "How did you work together as a team?"

**Key Points to Make:**
- "Great teamwork! You had to work together to figure it out"
- "You learned something about your den members"
- "Communication isn't just about talking - body language matters too!"
- "This is what Cub Scouting is about - working together and having fun!"

---

## Tips for Success

✅ **Let Them Struggle a Bit:** Don't jump in too quickly - figuring it out is part of the fun!

✅ **Keep the Energy High:** Make it exciting! "This is going to be tricky!" "Can you do it?"

✅ **No Peeking When They're Lining Up:** Make sure scouts aren't helping by talking!

✅ **Adjust Difficulty:** 
   - Younger/shy scouts: Stick to easier challenges (birth month, height)
   - Confident scouts: Try harder ones (number of letters, street address number)

✅ **Celebrate Effort:** Even if they don't get it perfect, praise the teamwork!

✅ **Watch for Exclusive Behavior:** Make sure all scouts are included and helping each other

## Challenge Ideas (Pick Based on Your Group)

### Easier Challenges:
- Birth month
- Height (can see it!)
- Alphabetically by first name
- Age

### Medium Challenges:
- Alphabetically by last name
- Number of siblings
- Shoe size

### Harder Challenges:
- Number of letters in first name
- House/apartment number
- Distance they live from school
- Birthday (month AND day)

### Super Hard (if time allows and they want a challenge):
- Complete birthday including year (oldest to youngest)
- Middle name alphabetically
- Favorite number (they have to show with fingers)

## Common Questions & Answers

**Q: "Can we write on the ground/in the air?"**  
A: Yes! Any non-verbal communication is allowed.

**Q: "What if someone is talking?"**  
A: Gentle reminder: "Remember, no talking! Use gestures!" Reset if needed.

**Q: "We can't figure it out!"**  
A: Give a small hint: "Try using your fingers to show numbers" or "Point to someone who should go first"

**Q: "This is too easy!"**  
A: Great! Try a harder challenge from the list above.

**Q: "We don't have enough space"**  
A: You only need about 10-15 feet. Scouts can stand close together.

---

## Timing Guide

With **12 minutes per rotation:**
- 1 min: Welcome & explain
- 3 min: Round 1 (Birth Month)
- 3 min: Round 2 (First Name)
- 3 min: Round 3 (Your Choice)
- 2 min: Debrief & discussion

**If Running Ahead:**
- Do another challenge!
- Try a super hard one
- Let them pick the next challenge

**If Running Behind:**
- Skip Round 3
- Shorten debrief
- Move quickly between rounds

---

## Rotation Transition

**When Time is Called:**
1. Wrap up quickly: "Great job, everyone!"
2. Thank the scouts for their teamwork
3. Reset your space (make sure it's clear)
4. Welcome the next group with fresh energy!

---

## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** Scouts are talking/cheating  
**Solution:** "Oops! I heard talking! Let's start over and try to do it completely silent this time."

**Problem:** One scout is dominating/bossing  
**Solution:** Remind them: "Everyone needs to work together. Let everyone share their ideas."

**Problem:** A scout feels left out or confused  
**Solution:** Pause and say: "Make sure everyone understands what we're doing!"

**Problem:** They finish WAY too fast  
**Solution:** Keep going! Do more challenges. The harder ones will slow them down.

**Problem:** Too much physical contact/pushing  
**Solution:** "Gentle hands, please! We can point and gesture without touching."

---

## What Success Looks Like

✅ Scouts are laughing and having fun  
✅ Everyone is participating  
✅ Creative communication (gestures, miming, etc.)  
✅ Teamwork and helping each other  
✅ Learning facts about den members  

---

## Closing Note
End each rotation with one quick debrief question ("What was hardest?" or "How did you show without words?"). Then clear space and greet next group promptly.
