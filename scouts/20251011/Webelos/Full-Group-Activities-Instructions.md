# Full Group Activities - Den Leader Instructions

**Time:** 15 minutes total (after station rotations complete)  
**Group Size:** All 18 scouts  
**Your Role:** Den Leader

---

## Overview

After the station rotations, you'll lead three full-group activities that complete the remaining Bobcat requirements. These activities bring everyone together and use what they learned at the stations.

---

## Materials Needed

- [ ] 2 large posterboards (22" x 28")
  - One labeled "Our Den Code of Conduct"
  - One labeled "Denner Responsibilities"
- [ ] 8-10 colorful markers
- [ ] Collected papers from Station 3 (Denner Brainstorming)
- [ ] Webelos handbook (for reference)

---

## Activity 1: Recite Scout Oath & Law (8 minutes)
**Requirement 2**

### Setup
- Gather all scouts in a circle or rows
- Have everyone standing

### Instructions

**Step 1: Practice the Cub Scout Sign (2 minutes)**
"Let's start by making the Cub Scout sign - you just learned this at Station 2!"
- Have everyone raise their right hand with index and middle fingers up
- Check that they're doing it correctly
- "This is how we show we're ready to recite the Scout Oath and Law"

**Step 2: Recite the Scout Oath (3 minutes)**
"Now we'll recite the Scout Oath together. I'll lead, and you repeat after me."

**Scout Oath:**
```
On my honor I will do my best
To do my duty to God and my country
and to obey the Scout Law;
To help other people at all times;
To keep myself physically strong,
mentally awake, and morally straight.
```

**Step 3: Recite the Scout Law (2 minutes)**
"Now let's recite the Scout Law together. You practiced this at Station 1!"

**Scout Law:**
```
A Scout is trustworthy, loyal, helpful, friendly,
courteous, kind, obedient, cheerful, thrifty,
brave, clean, and reverent.
```

**Step 4: Discuss the 3 Points (2 minutes)**
"The Scout Oath has three important parts. Let's talk about them."

**Quickly Review the Three Points:**
1. **Duty to God & Country** - Following your faith, being a good citizen
2. **Duty to Other People** - Helping others, doing Good Turns daily
3. **Duty to Self** - Physically strong, mentally awake, morally straight

**Quick Examples:**
- "When you help a friend, that's duty to other people"
- "When you do your homework, that's keeping yourself mentally awake"
- "When you're honest, that's being morally straight"

## Activity 2: Create Den Code of Conduct (4 minutes)
**Requirement 4**

### Setup
- Posterboard labeled "Our Den Code of Conduct"
- Markers available
- Scouts gathered where they can see

### Instructions

**Introduce the Concept (30 seconds)**
"A Code of Conduct is a set of rules for how we treat each other in our den. These are OUR rules that WE create together."

**Brainstorm Rules (1 minute)**
"What rules should we have?"

**Guide Quickly Toward 4-5 Rules:**
- Respect others
- Listen when someone is talking
- Help each other
- Be kind
- Have fun!

**Write & Sign (2 minutes)**
- Quickly write 4-5 rules on the posterboard
- Have all scouts sign their names
- "This poster will hang at every meeting!"

---

## Activity 3: Finalize Denner Responsibilities (3 minutes)
**Requirement 5**

### Setup
- Posterboard labeled "Denner Responsibilities"
- Collected papers from Station 3
- Markers

### Instructions

**Review Ideas (1 minute)**
"At Station 3, you wrote down ideas for what a denner should do."
- Quickly read the most common ideas from collected papers

**Vote on Top 5 (1 minute)**
"Let's vote on the 5 most important jobs!"
- Read options, scouts raise hands
- Tally quickly and choose top 5

**Write & Explain (1 minute)**
- Write the 5 responsibilities on posterboard
- "When you're denner, you'll wear a yellow cord"
- "Everyone will get a turn!"
- Announce first denner or rotation schedule

---

## Tips for Success

✅ **Keep Energy High:** These are 18 kids after stations - keep it moving!

✅ **Use the Cub Scout Sign:** When you need attention, raise your hand in the sign

✅ **Be Efficient:** These are quick activities - don't let them drag

✅ **Celebrate Participation:** "Great thinking!" "I love that idea!"

---

## Time Adjustments

**If Running Ahead:**
- Let scouts decorate the posters
- Discuss Scout Law points in more detail
- Have scouts share examples of the Scout Oath points

**If Running Behind:**
- Skip poster decorations
- You write rules quickly without much discussion
- Skip announcing first denner (do it via email later)
